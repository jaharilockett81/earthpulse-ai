        How EarthPulse works

        A fixed eight-stage pipeline. No agent loops, no invented places, and every number traceable.

        1. Understand your favourites, or parse your description (rules, or an optional LLM that may only copy words you used).
        2. Match each interest to a Qloo entity via search, or to a Qloo taste tag for concepts like “slow living”.
        3. Portrait: Qloo's tag insights summarise the themes and vibes your favourites share.
        4. Discover: one Qloo place-insights call per category, scoped to your destination and signalled by your taste, with explainability on.
        5. Evidence: straight-line distance from your start point, and round-trip CO2e by mode from the U.S. EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub (2025, Table 10).
        6. Plan: score = Qloo affinity − distance penalty (if low-carbon travel is on) + small boosts from the place's own Qloo tags. Lighter swaps are proposed when a much closer place is nearly as good a taste match.
        7. Verify: each stop is re-checked against its own tags (category, not closed, budget, step-free) and every CO2e figure is recomputed. Failures are replaced.
        8. Explain: a one-line “why” assembled from grounded facts only.

        Scoring weights

        Distance penalty0.02 per km beyond the first km, capped at 0.25 (0.03 / 0.35 if you won't drive)
        More time in nature+0.05 for parks and gardens
        Plant-forward food+0.06 when the place's Qloo tags mention vegan, vegetarian or plant-based
        Secondhand & vintage+0.06 when its tags mention thrift, vintage or used
        Lighter swapSame category, base score within 0.06, at most 60% of the distance and at least 1 km closer

        Emission factors

        Passenger car0.2986 kg CO2e per vehicle-mile (divided by travellers)
        Bus0.0666 kg CO2e per passenger-mile
        Transit rail0.0935 kg CO2e per passenger-mile

        Source: U.S. EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub, 2025, Table 10; CO2e uses the Hub's AR5 GWPs (CH4 = 28, N2O = 265). Distances are straight-line, so figures are lower bounds and meant for comparison. Lower-impact tags are Qloo taste signals, not certifications.
